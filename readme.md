# Awesome Digital Nomads [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated, actively maintained list of tools and resources for living and working from anywhere — finance, visas, insurance, eSIMs, communities, and more.

[![IndepAI — AI-powered FIRE planning for digital nomads](https://indepai.app/og-image.png)](https://indepai.app/coming-soon)

Maintained by the team behind [IndepAI](https://indepai.app), the financial-independence app for digital nomads: free FIRE calculators, cost-of-living data for 11,400+ cities, and 180+ tax regimes. Every link here is checked weekly by CI; pull requests get reviewed, not abandoned.

## Finance & FIRE

- [IndepAI](https://indepai.app/tools) - FIRE planning built for nomads: FI score, geo-arbitrage math across 11,400+ cities, and 11 free calculators including Coast FIRE and Barista FIRE. From the maintainers of this list.

- [ProjectionLab](https://projectionlab.com/) - Retirement and financial-independence planner with Monte Carlo projections, cash-flow modeling, and tax presets for several countries plus a selectable display currency.
- [cFIREsim](https://www.cfiresim.com/) - Runs your portfolio and spending plan against historical market cycles to show how often it would have survived a full retirement.
- [FI Calc](https://ficalc.app/) - Backtests retirement withdrawal strategies against decades of historical market returns.
- [Kubera](https://www.kubera.com/) - Net-worth tracker that aggregates banks, brokerages, crypto, and real estate across currencies and international institutions.
- [Sharesight](https://www.sharesight.com/) - Portfolio and dividend tracker that consolidates holdings across global stock exchanges and reports performance and tax figures.
- [Ghostfolio](https://ghostfol.io/) - Open-source, self-hostable portfolio tracker for stocks, ETFs, and crypto.
- [Exirio](https://www.exirio.com/) - Wealth tracker that consolidates investments, crypto, and property across more than 40 currencies.
- [Snowball Analytics](https://snowball-analytics.com/) - Dividend and portfolio tracker covering 70+ exchanges and 25+ currencies, with a dividend calendar and benchmarking.
- [YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/) - Zero-based budgeting app built around assigning every unit of money a job, usable with whatever currency you set.
- [CAGR Calculator](https://nutilz.com/cagr-calculator) - Free browser tool that computes compound annual growth rate from a start value, end value, and holding period, useful for comparing brokerage or portfolio returns across different currencies and time horizons without a spreadsheet.

## Cost of Living Data

- [IndepAI City Data](https://indepai.app/cities) - Cost-of-living data for 11,400+ cities tied directly to FIRE math: see what your savings buy in each city. From the maintainers of this list.
- [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/) - Crowd-sourced database of prices, rent, and quality-of-life indices for thousands of cities, with side-by-side comparisons.
- [Expatistan](https://www.expatistan.com/) - Compares the cost of living between two cities using prices submitted by residents and expats.
- [Nomad List](https://nomads.com/) - Ranks cities for remote workers by cost of living, internet speed, safety, and weather, alongside community features.
- [Livingcost.org](https://livingcost.org/) - Cost-of-living calculator and rankings covering more than 9,000 cities across 197 countries.
- [Budget Your Trip](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/) - Crowd-sourced average daily travel costs by destination and travel style, drawn from tens of thousands of travelers.
- [ReloMap](https://relomap.app/) - Compares cost of living, taxes, and safety across 200+ destinations, with a tax calculator and visa guides.

## Banking & Money Transfer

- [Wise](https://wise.com/) - Multi-currency account holding 40+ currencies with local bank details, a debit card, and transfers at the mid-market rate.
- [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/) - App-based multi-currency account and card with in-app exchange across dozens of currencies and fee-free ATM allowances.
- [N26](https://n26.com/) - Mobile bank with a German banking licence, offering accounts and cards across 24 European markets.
- [bunq](https://www.bunq.com/) - Dutch mobile bank with multi-IBAN accounts, sub-accounts, and travel features like in-app eSIMs.
- [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com/) - Account for receiving international client and marketplace payments in multiple currencies across 190+ countries.
- [Remitly](https://www.remitly.com/) - Money-transfer service for sending remittances to recipients in 175+ countries.
- [OFX](https://www.ofx.com/) - International money transfers for individuals and businesses to 180+ countries, with multi-currency accounts.
- [XE](https://www.xe.com/) - Live exchange-rate data, a currency converter, and international transfers to 190+ countries.
- [Banking Access Index](https://www.globalsolo.global/data/banking-access-index) - Free CC-BY dataset tracking which of 18 US banking providers accept non-US-resident business owners across 8 countries, with evidence-verified policy claims and CSV/JSON downloads.

## Taxes & Legal

- [Bright!Tax](https://brighttax.com/) - US expat tax firm handling federal returns, FBAR/FATCA reporting, and streamlined catch-up filing for Americans abroad.
- [Taxes for Expats](https://www.taxesforexpats.com/) - CPA-prepared US tax returns for Americans abroad, dual citizens, and green-card holders, including streamlined and FBAR filings.
- [Greenback Expat Tax Services](https://www.greenbacktaxservices.com/) - US expat tax preparation pairing clients with dedicated CPAs and enrolled agents for returns, FBAR, and late-filing catch-up.
- [doola](https://www.doola.com/) - US LLC and C-corp formation with EIN, registered agent, bookkeeping, and tax filing for non-resident founders.
- [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas) - Incorporate a US company (Delaware C-corp or LLC), get an EIN, and file the 83(b) election from 140+ countries.
- [Firstbase](https://www.firstbase.io/) - Form and run a US company with incorporation, compliance, a virtual mailroom, and bookkeeping, aimed at international founders.
- [Xolo](https://www.xolo.io/) - Run a solo EU company through Estonian e-Residency, or invoice clients without registering one, with accounting handled.
- [Elcano](https://elcano.tax/) - Tracks days spent per country against tax-residency thresholds, including the 183-day rule, Schengen 90/180, the UK Statutory Residence Test, and the US Substantial Presence Test.
- [LLC Class](https://llcclass.com/) - Forms Wyoming LLCs for non-US residents, bundling the state filing, a year of registered-agent service, and EIN application guidance.
- [Your Tax Base](https://yourtaxbase.com/) - Helps US mobile workers set up Florida domicile to avoid state income tax, handling the Florida address, Declaration of Domicile, and supporting paperwork.
- [Radar Fiscal](https://radarfiscal.es) - Free calendar of Spanish autónomo tax deadlines (Modelo 303, 130, 111, Seguridad Social) showing each filing window and due date, with a Chrome and Firefox extension for reminders.

- [Cigna Global](https://www.cignaglobal.com/) - International private health insurance for expats and globally mobile workers, with customizable plans and multilingual support.
- [IMG](https://www.imglobal.com/) - Travel medical and international health plans for travelers and expats, with evacuation and assistance services.
- [Heymondo](https://heymondo.com/) - Travel insurance with medical coverage and an app for filing claims and reaching 24/7 assistance.

## Visas & Residency

- [Citizen Remote](https://citizenremote.com/) - Relocation platform with a searchable digital nomad visa database plus tax, insurance, and housing help across 30+ countries.
- [Nomads Embassy](https://nomadsembassy.com/) - Digital nomad visa guides and a free eligibility check for dozens of countries, with paid application assistance.
- [iVisa](https://www.ivisa.com/) - Online service for checking entry rules and applying for tourist visas, eVisas, and travel documents.
- [VisaHQ](https://www.visahq.com/) - Visa requirement lookup by nationality and destination, with optional handling of the consulate application process.
- [Global Citizen Solutions](https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/) - Advisory firm for residency and citizenship by investment, including golden visas and its own Digital Nomad Visa Index.
- [Nomad Capitalist](https://nomadcapitalist.com/) - Consultancy for second residency, citizenship, and legal tax planning for people who live across borders.
- [Get Golden Visa](https://www.getgoldenvisa.com/) - Residency and citizenship-by-investment consultancy covering programs in Portugal, Greece, Malta, and the Caribbean.

## Trip Planning

- [IndepAI Nomad Planner](https://indepai.app/tools/geo-arbitrage) - Plans multi-city moves around visa limits and shows how each stop changes your cost of living and financial-independence date. From the maintainers of this list.

- [Where Do I Move To](https://wheredoimoveto.com/) - Free quiz that recommends countries and cities to relocate to based on cost of living, jobs, safety, and lifestyle preferences.
- [Hackamaps](https://hackamaps.com/) - Map-based directory for finding hackathons worldwide, aimed at planning travel around coding events.
- [Famxplor](https://www.famxplor.com/) - Finds family-tested vacation activities by destination, drawn from firsthand travel-blog accounts by parents traveling with kids.
- [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/) - Free multi-city itinerary builder with map routing, booking imports, and group collaboration.
- [TripIt](https://www.tripit.com/) - Turns forwarded booking confirmations into one organized itinerary you can view offline and share.

## Flights & Transport

- [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) - Flight search with a flexible-date grid, price tracking, and a map view for finding cheap destinations.
- [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.net/) - Flight, hotel, and car search that can scan a whole country or "everywhere" for the cheapest options.
- [Kiwi.com](https://www.kiwi.com/) - Flight booking that stitches separate airlines into one itinerary, handy for multi-city and open-jaw routes.
- [Going](https://www.going.com/) - Email alerts for cheap flights, fare sales, and mistake fares from your chosen home airports; formerly Scott's Cheap Flights.
- [Jack's Flight Club](https://www.jacksflightclub.com/) - Subscription that emails hand-picked flight deals and error fares for European and North American departure points.
- [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/) - Journey planner showing how to travel between any two places by plane, train, bus, ferry, or car, with fares and booking links.
- [Trainline](https://www.thetrainline.com/) - Booking site for train and coach tickets across 270+ operators in the UK and Europe.
- [12Go](https://12go.asia/) - Booking platform for trains, buses, ferries, and transfers, with deep coverage across Southeast Asia.

## Accommodation

- [Flatio](https://www.flatio.com/) - Deposit-free furnished rentals from five days to a year, aimed at remote workers and slow travelers.
- [HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com/) - Marketplace for mid- and long-term rentals in 400+ cities, with payment held until move-in.
- [Blueground](https://www.theblueground.com/) - Move-in-ready furnished apartments rented by the month in cities worldwide.
- [Anyplace](https://www.anyplace.com/) - Monthly furnished rentals built around remote work, with dedicated workspaces and fast internet.
- [Spotahome](https://www.spotahome.com/) - Furnished mid-term rentals across European cities, booked online from verified listings without in-person viewings.
- [Coliving.com](https://coliving.com/) - Global directory and booking site for coliving spaces with furnished rooms and shared amenities.
- [FindYourColiving](https://findyourcoliving.com/) - Searchable directory of over 1,000 coliving spaces across 343 cities and 97 countries, with prices and amenities.
- [Outsite](https://www.outsite.co/) - Membership network of coliving houses in remote-work destinations, from Lisbon to Bali.
- [TrustedHousesitters](https://www.trustedhousesitters.com/) - Membership marketplace pairing travelers with homeowners for free stays in exchange for pet and house sitting.

## Connectivity

- [Airalo](https://www.airalo.com/) - eSIM marketplace with local, regional, and global prepaid data plans for 200+ countries.
- [Nomad](https://www.nomadesim.com/) - Travel eSIM app selling prepaid data across 200+ destinations, with top-ups and no roaming charges.
- [Ubigi](https://www.ubigi.com/) - eSIM data provider for phones, tablets, and laptops, run by telecom operator Transatel.
- [eSIMdb](https://esimdb.com/) - Independent comparison database indexing travel eSIM plans from 170+ providers by price, data, and coverage.
- [PikaSim](https://pikasim.com/) - Travel eSIM sold with no account, name, or ID, covering 190+ countries and accepting card or cryptocurrency payment.
- [YohoMobile](https://www.yohomobile.com/) - Travel eSIM with prepaid data plans for 200+ countries, including multi-country and build-your-own options.
- [WiFi Map](https://www.wifimap.io/) - Crowdsourced app for finding free WiFi hotspots worldwide, with travel eSIM data as an add-on.
- [Instabridge](https://instabridge.com/) - WiFi finder with millions of shared hotspots plus eSIM data for 190+ countries.
- [Phonecall.app](https://phonecall.app/) - Calls landlines and mobiles in over 200 countries from a web browser, billed per second from a prepaid balance, with no app or SIM required.
- [DialHard](https://dialhard.com/) - Browser calling to phone numbers worldwide over WebRTC with pay-per-minute credit and rentable local numbers; handy for reaching banks or government lines from abroad.
- [UK SIM Keep-Number Reminder](https://getgiffgaff.com/tools/keep-number-reminder/) - Creates an early fifth-month calendar reminder for an infrequently used giffgaff SIM; runs locally, exports an ICS file, and stores no phone number or account details.
- [eSIMzio](https://esimzio.com/) - Travel eSIM app for 200+ destinations that shows real prices up front, live data usage read from the carrier, and each plan’s fair-usage limit before you buy. Available for iOS and Android.
- [Simnity](https://simnity.com/) - Travel eSIM for 190+ countries that pairs prepaid 5G data with a local number for voice calls and SMS, alongside a device-compatibility checker and plans priced in USD or INR.

## Communities

- [r/digitalnomad](https://www.reddit.com/r/digitalnomad/) - Reddit's main remote-work forum, 2.3 million members trading advice on cities, visas, and taxes.
- [WiFi Tribe](https://wifitribe.co/) - Membership community running month-long co-living and co-working trips across several continents.
- [Hacker Paradise](https://www.hackerparadise.org/) - Small-group work-and-travel programs of 2 to 4 weeks for developers, designers, and remote workers.
- [Nomad Cruise](https://www.nomadcruise.com/) - Recurring conference-at-sea sailings mixing talks, workshops, and networking for nomads and founders.
- [Remote Year](https://remoteyear.com/) - Organizes 1- to 12-month group trips where members keep their jobs while housing and workspace are handled.
- [Freaking Nomads](https://freakingnomads.com/) - Nomad platform combining a destination and coworking directory, an events board, discussion forums, and a resources index.
- [Punta.app](https://punta.app/) - Mobile app that surfaces other nomads heading to the same destination so you can connect before arrival, with location-based hubs once there.

- [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/) - Board with over 100,000 live roles plus free tools for profiles, resumes, and applications.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) - Startup job marketplace (formerly AngelList Talent) where you apply to founders with pay and equity shown upfront.
- [Dynamite Jobs](https://dynamitejobs.com/) - Fully remote roles at remote-first companies, tied to the Dynamite Circle founder community.
- [Contra](https://contra.com/) - Commission-free marketplace for hiring and paying freelancers in design, development, and creative work.
- [Remote Nomad Jobs](https://www.remotenomadjobs.com/) - Job board of fully remote, work-from-anywhere roles with category and salary filters.
- [AI Dev Jobs](https://aidevboard.com/) - Job board for AI and machine-learning roles with remote filters, salary data, and a public API for querying listings.
- [4DayJob](https://4dayjob.com/) - Job board dedicated to four-day-week and reduced-hours roles, filterable by category, location, and region.
- [FoundRole](https://www.foundrole.com/) - Free job-search tool pairing an hourly-updated board and remote filters with a kanban application tracker.
