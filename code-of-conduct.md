# Code of Conduct

Be excellent to each other.

- Review suggestions on their merits; product owners may submit their own work
  as long as they disclose it.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks — in issues, PRs, or commit
  messages.
- Disagreements about whether a resource belongs here are settled by the
  maintainers; lobbying via sockpuppet accounts gets you banned.
- Spam PRs (affiliate farms, keyword-stuffed descriptions, link-scheme
  submissions) are closed without discussion and repeat offenders blocked.

Report problems to hello@indepai.app or by opening an issue. Maintainers will
respond within a week.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
